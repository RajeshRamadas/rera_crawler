🟢 LOW RISK — BUY GATE PASSED

🟠 MEDIUM RISK — LEGAL REVIEW

Potential discrepancy or missing historical document identified; advocate verification required.

🔴 HIGH RISK — DO NOT PROCEED

Material title mismatch, unreleased mortgage, PTCL/lake buffer violation, or court litigation detected.

⚫ INCONCLUSIVE — INSUFFICIENT DATA
