Verification Standard

The 9-Pillar Legal Due-Diligence Architecture

Every project is cross-checked against Karnataka land legislation, municipal rules, and court precedents.

📜 1. Title & Ownership Chain

Continuous 30+ year registered title chain, Form 16 Encumbrance Certificate, Executant ID verification at SRO.

Sec 17, 32 & 34 Registration Act 1908
🌾 2. Revenue & Land Records

Bhoomi Pahani Col 9/11 owner match, Mutation Register MR Form 12, SSLR Podi 11E sketch, Akarband.

Sec 128 & 129 Karnataka Land Revenue Act
🏗️ 3. Land Conversion & Extent

Deputy Commissioner Non-Agricultural Use Conversion Order, converted acreage vs claimed site extent match.

Sec 95 Karnataka Land Revenue Act 1964
📐 4. Layout & Planning Sanction

BDA / BMRDA / DTCP Sanctioned Layout Plan, NOPA Site Release Certificate, BDA Master Plan 2031 zoning.

Sec 15 & 17 KTCP Act 1961 / BDA Act 1976
🏢 5. RERA Compliance & Filings

RERA Registration Certificate validity, Form B Promoter Declaration, Bank Escrow Affidavit, CA Form 3 audit.

Sec 4(2)(l) RERA Act 2016
🏛️ 6. Municipal Khata & Tax

BBMP e-Aasthi e-Khata status, active PID number, Self-Assessment Scheme (SAS) property tax paid receipts.

Sec 114 & 115 KMC Act / BBMP Act 2020
⚖️ 7. Courts & Litigation Search

e-Courts suit search across Karnataka High Court & City Civil Courts, Advocate Title Search Report (TSR).

Order XXXIX CPC & Lis Pendens Sec 52 TPA
🏦 8. Encumbrances & Mortgages

CERSAI security registry mortgage search, Bank Mortgage NOC, lender project escrow charge check.

Sec 26D SARFAESI Act 2002
🚫 9. Restricted & Special Land

B-Kharab Govt Public Land, PTCL SC/ST Grant Act non-alienation, NGT 75m lake buffer zone, GIS satellite check.

PTCL Act 1978 & NGT OA 222/2014 Buffer
